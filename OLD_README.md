# Mechanized Proof of a Subset of Typed Assembly Language


## asm_jmp_bnz

A "simple typed assembly language" (we refer to it sometimes as STAL) in the style of TAL of Morrisett et al. [1] and derived from simplification of TAL of Morrisett et al. [1], with addition/multiplication, load/store, move, malloc, conditional and unconditional jumps.  
</br>
The mechanized proof of <b>asm_jmp_bnz</b> has been automatically generated by [Lang-n-Prove](https://github.com/mcimini/lang-n-prove-state/).

[1] [From System F to Typed Assembly Language](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~dpw/papers/tal-toplas.pdf). Greg Morrisett, David Walker, Karl Crary, Neal Glew. TOPLAS, Volume 21, Issue 3, Pages 527 - 568, 1999.

## tal-minus

A Typed Assembly Language: TAL of Morrisett et al. [1] without existential types and where <b>malloc</b> are initializing. 
</br>
The mechanized proof of <b>tal-minus</b> has been obtained by editing the proof of <b>asm_jmp_bnz</b>. 
</br>
The <b>tal-minus.thm</b> proof contains comments were modifications/additions have been needed. These comments have the form <b>/* TAL1: ... */</b>.
</br>
</br>
<i><b>Time effort</b></i>: ~2 work days and a half (starting from <b>asm_jmp_bnz</b>).
